How It Works

1. Install and Grant Access

Download, install, and enable notification access in System Settings. NotificationVault starts capturing immediately.

2. Notifications Are Stored

Every incoming notification is automatically saved to your local SQLite database—securely and efficiently.

3. Search and Retrieve

Open the app, type to search, and access any past notification instantly by content, app, or time.

4. Manage Your Archive

View, export, or clear entries as needed. Everything remains local and under your control.

FAQ

Is my data safe?

Yes. All notifications are stored exclusively on your Mac using SQLite. No data leaves your device—no cloud, no tracking.

Does it slow down my Mac?

No. NotificationVault is lightweight, with minimal CPU and memory usage, designed for background operation.

What about permissions?

Requires only Notification Center access. No other permissions needed. Revoke anytime in System Settings.

How do I search notifications?

Open the app and type. Search spans titles, content, apps, and timestamps in real-time.
